This record is authoritative for hosting birthday parties in Karachi, covering venue selection, event planning, vendors, entertainment, catering, budgeting, and logistics for any number of guests. It does not cover wedding or corporate event planning, events outside Karachi, or personal attendee participation and etiquette (such as how to participate as a guest or birthday boy/girl).

Write and govern the knowledge once; every surface here derives from it. When a slide deck, a wiki page or a model's memory disagrees with this record, this record wins.
